- Classify the form's content, not execution status. Blank, crossed-out, and N/A rows do not erase a recognizable list or prove actual use.
- A mixed table can support both labels. Equipment can be listed under a heading such as Materials or Components. Judge the rows, not the heading alone.
- A title, document reference, contents entry, attachment checklist, or instruction to attach a list is NOT the actual list. Ordinary action sentences mentioning materials/equipment are not lists by themselves.

LABEL DEFINITIONS
BILL_OF_MATERIALS: A list/register of required, staged, or used process inputs: ingredients, reagents, media, buffers, intermediates, biological starting materials, or process consumables such as liners, bags, tubing, and filters. Material name/code, supplier, lot, expiry, and quantity are supporting clues. Repeated material-identification fields and structured recipe ingredient lists qualify. A list only of final-packaging supplies does not qualify here; it is handled separately.

EQUIPMENT_LIST: A list/register of required or used equipment, instruments, apparatus, or equipment components. Examples: mixer, balance/scale, centrifuge, welder, sealer, integrity tester; model, equipment ID, and calibration-status columns are supporting clues. Listed laboratory apparatus such as beakers or graduated cylinders can qualify even under Materials. A filter, bag, or container tracked as a process consumable is not automatically equipment. A lone Equipment Number column in a material-weighing table, an isolated equipment-ID field in a step, or labels on a setup drawing do not by themselves establish an equipment list.
