LABEL DEFINITIONS
BILL_OF_MATERIALS: An actual list/register of required, staged, or used process inputs: ingredients, reagents, media, buffers, intermediates, biological starting materials, or process consumables such as liners, bags, tubing, and filters. Material name/code, supplier, lot, expiry, and quantity are supporting clues, not mandatory fields. A structured recipe ingredient list or repeated ingredient-identification/quantity entries within an operating step qualifies. Ordinary action sentences mentioning ingredients do not. A list only of final-packaging supplies is handled separately.

EQUIPMENT_LIST: An actual itemized equipment register, identification block, or checklist identifying required/used equipment, instruments, apparatus, or components. It may be inside a procedure or clearance checklist; a structured single-item register qualifies. Equipment IDs, models, calibration status, and item-specific verification fields are clues. Listed laboratory apparatus such as beakers can qualify even under Materials. An action such as "Transfer to blender; ID: ___" is not by itself an equipment register. A lone Equipment Number column in a material table, a reference to an equipment list, or labels on an assembly drawing do not qualify. A consumable filter, bag, or container is not automatically equipment; judge its documented role.
