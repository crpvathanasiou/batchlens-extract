## 2. Fixed product boundary

**Stage 2 remains the independent, unchanged Lexical Extraction Engine.** Stage 3 invokes it and provides extraction-result review. Do not duplicate or redesign lexical extraction or L13.

Stage 1 document approval remains unchanged. Stage 3 approves extraction results, not source HTML.

Stage 3 has exactly one approval action:

**Approve extraction result**

It approves the complete saved extraction-review result currently presented for the selected document/run.

The current result has only two approval states:

- `not_approved`
- `approved`

Do not introduce:

- Page, component, lexical, LLM, or separate stage approvals.
- Individual finding approval or rejection.
- Approval dependencies, page invalidation, or retention machinery.
- `confirmed`, `waiting_for_approval`, `human_approved`, or `auto_approved` as Stage 3 review/finding lifecycle states.
- Automatic approval workflows or a generic approval-scope framework.

Existing Stage 1 states are outside this prohibition and must remain unchanged.

Keep these extraction-action mappings in the contract boundary, reusing existing Stage 2 definitions where appropriate:

| Action | Stage 2 preset |
|---|---|
| Extract UOs | `unit_operations_with_steps` |
| Extract Materials | `materials_with_quantities` |
| Extract Equipment | `equipment_with_parameters` |
| Extract All | `full` |

Fuzzy extraction is optional and disabled by default. Execution never grants approval.

## 4. Contract deliverables

Use the repository’s Python 3.11, Pydantic v2, typing, and validation conventions. Prefer small, explicit models over extensible frameworks. Version the persisted-data contracts that later tasks will consume.

### A. Source and run references

Represent enough immutable identity to bind a review to its existing source and extraction run:

- Reviewed-HTML identity and exact HTML-byte SHA-256.
- Existing review revision provenance.
- Run and published-manifest references.
- Knowledge snapshot identity.
- Requested/resolved components and recorded execution/publication outcomes.

Reuse Stage 2 value types when their meaning is identical. Do not import runtime execution or publication behavior into pure transitions.

Maintain these distinctions:

- `document_hash` is not the HTML-byte SHA-256.
- Reviewed HTML and the lexical manifest bind through reviewed-HTML provenance and SHA-256.
- Matching revision provenance in an approved review JSON does not prove a persisted export-pointer link when its `exports` field is null.
- Constructing a reference model does not verify files or hashes. Actual artifact verification belongs to later integration tasks.

Do not require `manifests-files.zip`.

### B. Review findings and edits

Keep separate:

- Immutable original extraction identity, matched text, evidence, and catalogue-candidate provenance.
- The current user-facing correction.
- Edit provenance, including `added_by_user`, `changed_by_user`, and `removed_by_user`.

A lexical finding remains source-labelled `lexical` after correction. A user edit does not create a semantic-confirmation lifecycle.

Support add, edit, and remove operations. Removal is a recorded content edit, not rejection; preserve its historical provenance.

Allow findings explicitly marked `no_document_evidence`. Allow no page assignment. Such findings belong to the future unassigned group and remain part of the complete result eligible for approval.

Never fabricate page, node, span, candidate, or source references. Preserve original evidence alongside corrections, including when corrected text differs from the source text.

Avoid copying potentially unbounded raw candidate/supporting-row collections into each review revision. Use immutable references and bounded review data.

### C. Saved revisions and current head

Define immutable saved review revisions and an explicit current head, with source/run references and parent linkage.

Keep revision changes bounded. Do not require full raw-artifact copies or full-history materialization for each save.

Distinguish content changes from approval metadata. Use an explicit, acyclic representation in which an approval records the exact already-saved review revision approved. If an approval transition advances the history/head, distinguish that transition’s identity from its target saved revision.

A later content revision must not inherit active approval merely through its parent linkage.

### D. Final approval

Define one concrete `ExtractionResultApproval`, recording:

- Actor.
- Server-provided timestamp.
- Exact saved review revision approved.
- Its document/source/run identity, directly or through validated immutable references.

Approval must not alter findings, component coverage, processing outcomes, or publication outcomes.

A completed subset, a valid published partial result, unassigned findings, and findings without document evidence can be approved. Approval does not assert that every possible extraction was performed.

### E. Mutation commands and outcomes

Define typed commands/results for initialization, saving bounded edits, and final approval.

Include:

- Expected current revision/head for conflict detection.
- Stable operation identity and sufficient command identity for later safe retry handling.
- Explicit validation, conflict, changed, and unchanged outcomes as appropriate.

Do not implement a durable retry ledger or storage service in U1.1. Define the contract needed for later tasks to distinguish an identical retry from reuse of an operation identity with different content.

Use finite, named bounds for mutation batches and user-controlled payload fields. Document their purpose and test the boundaries. Do not silently truncate Stage 2 evidence or results.

## 5. Pure transition behavior

Transitions must perform no filesystem, database, network, HTTP, worker, or clock operations. Supply actor, timestamp, identifiers, current head, and necessary resolved state as inputs.

Do not require loading an entire artifact or revision chain to process a bounded edit batch.

Implement and test these rules:

1. **Initialize:** A newly published, reviewable extraction result starts unapproved. Preserve its execution and publication facts, including completed components with zero findings.
2. **Save changes:** A valid content-changing save against the expected head produces a new unapproved revision and preserves the previous revision.
3. **No-op save:** Saving unchanged content preserves the current revision and approval.
4. **Approve:** Approval targets committed content only. The approval command cannot carry or silently save draft edits.
5. **Partial coverage:** Approval does not require all categories to have been requested or completed. It preserves failed, completed, and not-requested coverage.
6. **Edit after approval:** A subsequent committed content edit creates a new unapproved revision. The earlier revision and approval remain historical.
7. **New run:** A new extraction run creates a separate unapproved review. It does not accumulate findings or transfer edits or approval from an earlier run.
8. **Conflict:** A stale expected head returns a specific conflict outcome without changing state.
9. **Identity:** Reject inconsistent document, run, revision, or finding references using the available input facts.
10. **Read-only activity:** Viewing, navigation, and switching views are not content mutations and cannot invalidate approval.

Keep execution/publication outcomes and human approval independent. A failed unpublished run must not be converted into a reviewable published result by these transitions.

Specify deterministic behavior for repeated approval of the same unchanged saved result; do not create duplicate approval history through a no-op.

The browser UTF-16 versus Unicode-code-point offset issue remains a later implementation/test responsibility. Preserve existing Stage 2 span semantics. Do not solve browser conversion or redesign L13 here.

## 6. Focused automated checks

Tests must exercise domain behavior without filesystem, HTTP, SQLite, or browser fixtures.

Cover at least:

- Valid models, invalid identities, forbidden extra fields, and immutable nested contract data.
- Existing action-to-preset mappings and fuzzy default.
- Separation of HTML-byte SHA-256 from other document identity.
- Original matched text/evidence surviving user corrections.
- Add, edit, and remove provenance.
- Explicit no-evidence and unassigned findings.
- Bounded mutation validation, including the upper bound and one item beyond it.
- Initial completed-subset and published-partial reviews being unapproved.
- Successful approval of those results without changing coverage or outcomes.
- A completed zero-hit result remaining representable.
- Approval identifying the exact committed revision and rejecting mismatched targets.
- No draft-edit payload being accepted by the approval command.
- Edit after approval preserving historical approval while creating an unapproved revision.
- No-op save and repeated-approval behavior.
- Stale-head conflict with unchanged inputs.
- New-run isolation without automatic merging.
- Serialization round trips needed by the later persistence layer.

Do not add tests for removed approval models or speculative future workflows.
